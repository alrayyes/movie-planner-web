---
title: The viewing heatmap
description: A GitHub-contribution-style calendar of the days you went to watch something.
---

The "Calendar" page (in the nav) shows your whole logged history as a
GitHub-contribution-graph-style heatmap: one grid per calendar year,
the newest first, weeks running left to right as columns, Sunday-to-Saturday
as rows labelled Sun to Sat, and one small square per day. A day you
watched something is filled; every other day is empty, however many
films you saw. The Saturday and Sunday rows are a shade darker when
empty, so the weekend stands out. Hover or focus a day to see its count.

Click a filled day to see what you watched, in a popup listing each
viewing's title, showtime, medium, venue, and (when known) director,
genre, and IMDb rating — each title links straight to its own details
page. An empty day does nothing when clicked.

Click a year's own heading, or a month's own label over its columns, to
jump to the calendar overview filtered to that exact span.

Each year's grid runs its whole calendar year, January 1 through
December 31 (or through today, for the year still in progress) —
active or not, so a quiet stretch between viewings shows up as a run of
empty squares rather than being left out. Only years with at least
one logged viewing render at all.

This is a different view of the same data the calendar overview (the
home page) already shows as a filterable table — there's no filtering
or range picker here; use the overview's own From/To fields for a
narrower view.
